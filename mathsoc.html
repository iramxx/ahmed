"Math Behind" Series

A signature lecture series founded and led by Ahmed Maghri, designed to make complex ideas accessible without oversimplification.

  • • Math Behind AI, ML, & Neural Networks: Delivered by Ahmed Maghri – Moving from search algorithms to backpropagation, embeddings, and self-attention.
  • • Math Behind Cryptography, Cybersecurity, & QC: Delivered by Ahmed Maghri – Covering Number Theory, RSA, Shor's algorithm, and Lattice-based Cryptography.
  • • The Numbers Behind Trade: When Equations Meet Wall Street: Delivered by Ahmed Maghri & Adham – Bridging continuous models, Brownian motion, and the Black-Scholes PDE.
